           successive periods [s]: 2.2217, 2.1104, 2.0612, 2.0368, 2.0241, 2.0174 ... 2.0098, 2.0096, 2.0095

Time lag of nonlinear behind linear at the last common swing: 0.823 s (147 deg of phase)

Reading:
- Nonlinear period starts longer than Td and converges to Td as the amplitude decays
  (the frequencies synchronise) - this is physics: unchanged when dt is halved.
- The phase lag built up during the large-amplitude swings does NOT disappear,
  so the two curves keep a constant offset rather than coinciding exactly.
